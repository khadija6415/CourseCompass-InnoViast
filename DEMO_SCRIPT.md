# Demo Video Script (Target: 90 seconds)

Assignment requires the live demo to be understandable in under 2 minutes and free of credential leaks — so log in *before* recording starts, and keep DevTools/console closed throughout.

---

## [0:00–0:10] The problem

> "CS students waste hours hunting for YouTube tutorials that don't actually match their syllabus. CourseCompass fixes that — but Week 5's version only told you *what* to watch. It didn't help you track your progress or prove you actually learned it."

*(Screen: home page → pick a course/topic)*

## [0:10–0:25] Progress Tracking

> "Now, every resource has a status — Saved, In Progress, or Completed — and the topic page shows your overall progress."

*(Screen: open a resource → click "In Progress" → "Completed" → cut to topic page showing the progress bar update)*

## [0:25–0:50] Comprehension Quiz

> "Once you've completed a resource, you can test yourself — a short quiz pulls questions tagged to that exact topic, so it's not generic trivia, it's checking what this specific video actually covered."

*(Screen: click "Take the quiz" → answer 2–3 questions quickly → show results screen with score)*

## [0:50–1:05] Ratings & Reviews

> "Students can also rate and review resources directly, so the next person browsing knows if a video was actually worth their time before committing 40 minutes to it."

*(Screen: scroll to reviews section on a resource, show the star rating + a review)*

## [1:05–1:25] Analytics Dashboard

> "For the mentor or admin side, there's a full analytics dashboard — built entirely from real usage data, no separate tracking system. You can see completion rates, which resources are actually popular, and quiz performance by topic."

*(Screen: admin login → `/admin/analytics` → scroll through stat cards and the 14-day trend chart)*

## [1:25–1:30] Close

> "That's CourseCompass — from 'here's a video' to a platform that tracks, tests, and measures actual learning. Roadmap and full docs are in the repo."

*(Screen: back to home page or GitHub repo README)*

## Recording checklist

- [ ] Logged in as student *before* hitting record (no login screen with real credentials on camera)
- [ ] Browser zoom at 100%, DevTools console closed
- [ ] Have 2–3 pre-written quiz answers ready so you don't visibly hesitate on camera
- [ ] Do one full dry run first — this script is 90 seconds spoken at a normal pace, but screen transitions add time; trim wherever it runs long
- [ ] Export/upload as MP4, keep under the 1–2 minute assignment limit
